About

This website is a personal archive where I post an eclectic assortment of projects. Some are new, created with this website in mind, while some are old, and have either been adapted for this format or posted untouched. It started as a convenient way of hosting and sharing my Interview series, and the whole website remains a somewhat sporadically updated work in progress.

If you have any questions or comments, you can email me at finn.j.chapman@outlook.com or send me a message on Instagram. If I've sent you a link to this site or you've somehow stumbled across it, I hope it's been worth your time.
